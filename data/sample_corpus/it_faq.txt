Helio Labs IT FAQ

How do I reset my password?
Go to the Keystone self-service portal and choose "Forgot password". Resets require the Authenticator app. Passwords expire every 180 days and must be at least 14 characters.

What VPN do we use?
The company VPN is WireGuard-based and is called Tunnel. Install the Tunnel client from the internal software catalog. The VPN is mandatory when accessing the build farm from outside the office.

Who do I contact for laptop issues?
Open a ticket in the HelpDesk queue named "Hardware". The on-call IT technician responds within 4 business hours. Replacement laptops are issued from Building A, room 114.
