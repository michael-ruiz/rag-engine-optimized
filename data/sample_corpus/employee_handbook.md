# Helio Labs Employee Handbook

Helio Labs is a fictional 120-person robotics company used as sample data for patch-rag.

## Paid Time Off

Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of paid time off (PTO) per month, for 21 days per year.
Up to 5 unused PTO days roll over into the next calendar year; any balance above 5 days expires on January 31.
PTO requests longer than 3 consecutive days need manager approval at least two weeks in advance.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. The hardware lab in Building C requires on-site
presence on Tuesdays and Thursdays for anyone on the actuator team.

## Expense Policy

Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to $65 per day. Receipts are required for any single
expense above $25 and must be submitted within 30 days through the Ledger portal.
